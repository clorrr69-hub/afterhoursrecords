About

Built by artists, for artists.

After Hours Records is an independent label run out of Toronto, started by producer/engineer Constantin Tzenos (Odonis Odonis, After Hours Studio). It exists to put out music that doesn't fit neatly anywhere else — bold, textured, and made without a clock watching.
